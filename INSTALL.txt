GENZ GAMING CAFE — update bundle
=================================

WHAT'S NEW: MEMBER PRICES FOR FOOD
----------------------------------
Food can now have a member price, just like gaming does, and it applies by
itself when a member is on the station.

SETTING YOUR MEMBER PRICES
--------------------------
Food Menu > Edit on any item. There is a new box:

    Member price (Rs)     [ leave blank if members pay the same ]

Fill it in only where members pay less. Leave it blank on everything else
and those items simply cost what they always did — you do not need to touch
every item on the menu.

The menu screen shows both prices, so you can see at a glance which items
have a member deal:

    Burger          Rs 150
                    Rs 130 member

HOW IT WORKS AT THE COUNTER
---------------------------
Nothing to remember and nothing extra to tap.

When you start a session and add a member to the station, the Add food
screen for that station switches to member prices on its own. You will see
a green line at the top saying so, and every item with a member deal shows
the old price crossed out next to the member price:

    Coke        Rs 60  Rs 50        [ + Add ]
    Cold Coffee Rs 90  Rs 75        [ + Add ]
    Water       Rs 20               [ + Add ]     (no member deal — same price)

THE RULE FOR A SHARED PS5
-------------------------
If ANY of the players on that station is a member, the whole food order is
at member prices. That is what you chose, and it means a table of four with
one member is never an argument at the counter.

A FEW THINGS THAT ARE HANDLED FOR YOU
-------------------------------------
  * The price is decided by the system, on the server, from who is actually
    sitting at that station — not from anything the screen sends. Staff
    cannot get it wrong by accident.

  * The price is recorded onto the order the moment it is placed. If you
    change your menu prices next month, last month's bills do not move.

  * A member price higher than the normal price is refused as a typo.

  * An item with no member price is charged the same to everybody, always.

HOW TO INSTALL
--------------
1. Unzip this file.

2. Copy everything inside it into your genz-gaming-cafe folder, replacing
   the files when Windows asks. (Do NOT copy INSTALL.txt itself.)

3. Open PowerShell in that folder and run, one at a time:

       npm run db:migrate
       npx vercel --prod

4. Go to Food Menu and fill in the member prices where you offer them.

Nothing changes for your existing menu until you do step 4 — every item
starts with no member price, meaning everyone pays the same as today.
